## Style (from the reference video, colours from our app)

Borrow the reference's **motion, 3D staging, pacing and sound** only; never its colours, logo, wording, music or footage.

- Real app UI floating as 3D panels in a dark space: perspective camera, panels tilted, slow dolly and orbit.
- Exploded UI: one card lifts off the screen on the Z axis and fills the frame while the rest blurs.
- Layered parallax: background glow, mid UI layer, foreground cards.
- Micro-interactions: clicks, slider moves, numbers counting up, charts drawing in.
- Kinetic type between shots: 2 to 4 words, one accent word in emerald.
- Push-through transitions from one panel into the next screen.
- Sound effects on UI events, music ducked under the voice-over.
- Colour meaning, same as the app and poster: emerald = measured and met, amber = simulated, proposed or planned, muted red = missed.

Every screen shown exists in the real app; every number comes from `metrics/spark2026.json`; every simulated number carries an amber SIMULATED tag.

## Footage rules

- Pexels or Pixabay only (licences checked 25 Nov 2026: free, no attribution required, promotional use allowed). Mixkit not used unless its video licence is read and confirmed first.
- No clips with recognisable brands or logos. No identifiable face presented as the koperasi manager; the persona appears as hands, food and the counter.
- Every clip recorded in `docs/spark/FOOTAGE.md`: source page, licence, download date, scene.
- Stock clips set the mood only; they never stand in for app data or results.
