INSIGHTAID AI is an affordable web-based decision support system that helps campus cooperatives and small food businesses decide how much to prepare each day. It combines sales history with customer reviews in Bahasa Melayu, English and Mandarin and turns them into explainable actions. Its Prep & Stock Planner recommends tomorrow's quantity for every item, states the reason behind each number, and cuts the batch when customers complain that an item is stale ("basi"). Crisis alerts and rule-based recommendations show their evidence, and AI only words the explanation. Our XLM-RoBERTa model reached 84.12% accuracy across three languages, every analysis page loads in under three seconds, and a simulation on synthetic data cut food waste by 47.6%. Built on free-tier cloud and open-source models, it can be tried in one click with no sign-up.
