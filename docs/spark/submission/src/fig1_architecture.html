1. Data collectionuploaded as CSV

  • POS sales records
  • Google and Shopee reviews
  • Social posts (TikTok, X)
  • Languages: BM, English, Mandarin, code-mixed

2. Data processingsame pipeline for every upload

  • Template validation and duplicate check
  • Text cleaning
  • Language detection
  • Feature extraction, daily and weekly aggregates

3. Storagefree tier

  • Supabase PostgreSQL
  • Row-level security: each business sees only its own data
  • Raw rows kept for audit

4. Analytics engineexplainable by design

  • Multilingual sentiment
  • Sales forecasting
  • Crisis detection (negative share and its rate of rise)
  • Rule-based recommendations
  • Prep & Stock Planner

5. Decision dashboardweb app, < 3 s per page

  • Tomorrow's quantity per item, with the reason
  • Crisis alerts with evidence
  • Before vs After waste card
  • Model & Evidence page (results vs fixed targets)
Reviews reach the kitchen: when recent reviews name an item with a freshness complaint (e.g. “basi”), the planner cuts tomorrow’s batch and states why.
AI explains, rules decide: a language model only rewords the explanation of a recommendation; it never chooses the action or invents a number.
