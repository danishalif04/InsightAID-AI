## Description

```
INSIGHTAID AI turns sales records and multilingual customer reviews (Bahasa Melayu, English and Mandarin) into daily decisions for small businesses and campus cooperatives. Its Prep & Stock Planner says how much of each item to prepare tomorrow, and shows the reason for every number.

Try the demo, no sign-up: https://insightaid-ai.vercel.app/welcome
Source code: https://github.com/danishalif04/InsightAID-AI

Key results
• Multilingual sentiment model (XLM-RoBERTa): 84.1% accuracy on 10,769 test comments, above the 75% target in English, Bahasa Melayu and Mandarin (offline evaluation).
• Every analysis page loads in under 3 seconds (5 of 5 pages).
• Sales forecast met its accuracy target (MAPE under 15%) in 1 of 3 tests. We publish the misses too.
• Simulation on synthetic data: food waste RM 951.75 → RM 499.10 (47.6% less) over 28 days, with more stock-outs as the trade-off.

Silver Award, AIMIX: IID 2026 (Category B).

About the demo: Koperasi Siswa Maju is a fictional campus cooperative and all of its data is synthetic. Waste figures are a simulation, not a field result.
```
