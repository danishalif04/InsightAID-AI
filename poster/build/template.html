Methodology
  • Sentiment: 107,171 labelled comments (5 public sources); 85,639 train, 10,763 validation, 10,769 test, no overlap.
  • Forecasting: Olist sales, 470 days. Rolling-origin: train on the past, forecast the next h days, roll forward. MAPE, MAE, RMSE, MASE.
Findings & not yet evaluated
  • Sentiment input: no significant forecast gain ({{DM_WINS}} of {{N_DM}} DM tests, run {{RUN}}).
  • Code-mixed text not yet evaluated; live app uses a rating heuristic.
