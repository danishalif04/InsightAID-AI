# Voice-over script: the two pathway workflow videos

Two short videos, about 49 seconds each, with the same voice as the main demo video (female, warm, confident, unhurried, Malaysian English is welcome).

**Delivery:** a friendly product walkthrough, not an advert shout. Pause slightly at each comma.

**Pronunciation:**

- **InsightAid** is "insight-aid".
- **kedai** is "keh-die".
- **Kedai Makmur** is "keh-die mak-moor".

**Files:** one clip per line, WAV preferred (MP3 accepted), named exactly as below. Leave a little silence at each end, at most about 0.2 s.

- Video 1 clips go in `public/assets/vo-localshop/`
- Video 2 clips go in `public/assets/vo-startup/`

**Hard limit:** each clip must be **no longer than the "Max" column**, or it runs into the next scene. If a read is slightly long, speed it up a little rather than changing the words.

---
